# U.Center / U-topia FAQ (what John's assistant may say)

Draft built from U.Center call and explainer transcripts (Aug to Oct 2026). Kyle to confirm before the demo. Anything not written here, John answers personally.

## What U-topia is
- U-topia is a free money app for your phone. It puts what normally takes four apps into one: a crypto exchange, a wallet, an international money transfer app, and a bank-style account.
- You hold your own money. U-topia is non-custodial, which means U-topia can't touch your funds. It is not a bank, and it does not pay interest or give credit.
- It is built for normal people, not crypto experts. Most people figure it out in about ten minutes. You don't need to know anything about crypto.
- You can send money to someone's name (their U-tag) instead of a long wallet address, and transfers between users are gas-free.
- You can pay in shops with your phone (tap to pay) wherever Visa is accepted.
- You can hold different currencies, stablecoins, Bitcoin, and tokenized gold, all in one place.
- Physical cards are coming after launch, delivered to your home. You only pay shipping.
- At launch it works in more than fifty countries, with more being added.
- You must be eighteen or older to have an account.
- U-topia's founder and CEO is Emmanuel Quezada. The president is Kyle Kemper.

## What U.Center is
- U.Center is the community behind U-topia: an education and ambassador community, organized as a DAO. Instead of spending millions on ads, U-topia grows through people recommending it to people they know.
- Members get the U.Center Academy, a growing video library on money, crypto, financial tools, AI and entrepreneurship. They also get early access to the app and a community of people around the world.
- U.Center runs regular group discovery calls where newcomers can hear the whole story and ask questions. John is happy to bring people along.

## Joining U.Center
- You join by choosing a membership package through the referral link of the person who invited you. That person becomes your sponsor and helps you get started.
- It's a one-time payment for a lifetime membership. There's no monthly subscription.
- Packages start at one hundred dollars.
- How to join, step by step:
  1. Open your sponsor's referral link.
  2. Pick the package you're most comfortable with.
  3. Pay with USDT on the BNB Chain network. Double-check the address, the amount and the network before you send.
  4. Wait for the payment to be confirmed in U.Center.
  5. Start the Academy onboarding videos and join a discovery call. Your sponsor will walk you through it.
- John is happy to jump on a quick call, on WhatsApp or in person, to explain everything and help you set up.
