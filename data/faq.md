## U-topia in a nutshell
- U-topia is a free money app for your phone: "the financial ecosystem for a borderless world". Its line is "U-topia, built for you. Simple. Yours. Ready when you are."
- It puts four apps into one: a crypto exchange (like Binance), your own wallet (like MetaMask), an international money app (like Wise) and a bank-style account. One app, one experience, and no paying fees every time you move money between them.
- It connects everyday money and digital assets: you can hold currencies, stablecoins like USDT, Bitcoin and other crypto, tokenized gold and silver, and tokenized stocks, all in one place.
- It's built for normal people, not crypto experts. The founder's test is his own mum, who isn't a native English speaker and had never touched crypto: she should get it in about ten minutes. Nobody needs to know anything about crypto to start.
- U-topia has been five years in the making. U.Center members got the app first; it opens to everyone on 16 October 2026 at the Flow Summit in Bangkok.

## Your money stays yours
- U-topia is non-custodial: you hold your own keys and your own money. U-topia can't touch your funds, and it never lends out or trades customers' money. That's the big difference from exchanges like FTX, which collapsed with their customers' deposits.
- It is not a bank. It doesn't pay interest and doesn't offer credit or loans.
- If you ever lose access, wallet recovery runs through CoinCover, a UK company running since 2018, without U-topia ever holding your money.
- Transfers are approved with Face ID or a passkey. The wallet runs on Fireblocks technology, and the app got extra security layers, penetration testing and repeated audits before launch.
- The founder's own advice: keep your main money in your bank. Use U-topia to spend, to send money abroad, and to hold other currencies and assets.

## Sending money and U-tags
- Everyone picks a U-tag, a username. To send money you type someone's U-tag or pick them from your contacts, see their name and photo, and confirm. No long wallet addresses, so no money lost by a wrong digit or the wrong network.
- Sending between U-topia users is instant and free: no fees and no extra "gas" token needed. It works across networks like BNB Chain and Base, with Solana and Ethereum being added, and moving between networks is built in.
- It's made for families sending money home. Remittance shops can take ten percent or more; the founder's dad once paid around fourteen to seventeen percent sending him money from Mexico to Peru. Between U-topia users it's free and arrives in seconds.
- Premium U-tags go to the earliest sign-ups, first come first served.

## The card and paying in shops
- The U-topia card is a Visa card. You tap to pay with your phone anywhere Visa is accepted, through Apple Pay, Google Pay or Samsung Pay.
- You get a free virtual card instantly. Physical cards come after launch: you order one, it arrives at home in one to two weeks, and you only pay shipping. They're mainly useful for cash machines.
- Every payment sends an instant notification showing the amount in US dollars and in your local currency. You can freeze and unfreeze the card instantly and set your own spending limits.
- The card works in more than fifty countries at launch, and the app itself can be downloaded anywhere. Europe is next, around forty more countries including the UK and Switzerland, then more of Asia. The goal is more than a hundred countries by the end of 2027.

## Fees
- The app is free to download and use, with no monthly fee for individuals. The founder says he doesn't ever want to charge individuals one.
- Sending money between U-topia users is free.
- U-topia earns small fees when you swap or convert, for example from British pounds to US dollars or from crypto into a currency, and aims to keep them as low as possible. "Transparent fees, no surprises."
- The only other cost is shipping if you order a physical card.

## What you can hold
- Different currencies (the plan is 135), stablecoins like USDT, Bitcoin, Ethereum and other crypto, tokenized gold, tokenized silver (a BlackRock iShares silver product), and tokenized US stocks like Tesla, Amazon and NVIDIA through Kraken.
- You can start with a few dollars. Many people hold savings in a stronger currency than their own, for example a Mexican holding US dollars, as protection against inflation.
- It's your choice what you hold; U-topia doesn't tell anyone what to buy. Prices go up and down, and nothing here is financial advice.

## Coming next

- Business accounts for small businesses and self-employed people, so even a tuk-tuk driver can take payments in many currencies.
- U Bot, U-topia's own AI assistant built by Maya Nicks: you'll talk to your money ("what did I spend on food this month?"), and later it will make payments for you, like booking a flight when it's cheapest.

## U.Center, the community
- U.Center is the membership community behind U-topia: "the learning home of the U-topia ecosystem", where understanding comes first. Members learn together, help build and test U-topia, and help govern it.
- Instead of spending millions on ads, U-topia grows through people recommending it to people they know.
- U.Center is organized as a DAO, a community that runs itself by shared, open rules: transparent rules, decisions made by member votes, and a permanent public record. Governance features are switched on step by step as they're ready.
- The idea behind it is stakeholder capitalism: business that serves everyone it touches, not only shareholders. Members are stakeholders, not customers to extract value from.
- Education first: the U Academy library covers how money and wealth flow, stakeholder thinking, DAOs and governance, blockchain basics, crypto, AI and entrepreneurship, with quizzes, short "learn it, apply it" lessons and a twelve-part onboarding series. There's a U Kids section too.
- Members got the app before the public, plus a worldwide community, regular calls and events.
- The app is free for everyone. The membership is what you pay for.

## How to join, step by step
1. Open the referral link of the person who invited you. They become your sponsor and help you get started.
2. Pick your package, and choose whether it's a new membership, an upgrade, or a gift.
3. Pay with USDT on the BNB Chain network, also called Binance Smart Chain. USDT is the coin; BNB Chain is the network. When sending from an exchange, pick USDT as the asset and BNB Chain as the withdrawal network.
4. Double-check the address, the amount and the network before you send.
5. Wait for the payment to be confirmed in U.Center, then start the Academy onboarding videos and join a discovery call.
- Never done crypto before? That's normal: your sponsor can help you buy USDT and walk you through every step on a quick call.
- You must be eighteen or older.
